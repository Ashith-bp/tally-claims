# 5-minute pitch script: Tally Claims

Total: 5:00 maximum. Read it aloud once with a timer; trim the parts in [brackets] to your real story. Do not claim anything you cannot show.

## 1. Who you are (0:00 - 0:45)

"Hi, I'm **[your name]**, and I'm applying for the Founder's Office, Product & Growth role. **[1 sentence: what you do now / studied]**. **[1 sentence: the thing that makes you a builder or operator, e.g. a product you shipped, a team you ran, a growth channel you built]**. For this assignment I started a business called Tally Claims."

## 2. Your best work (0:45 - 1:15)

"The thing I'm proudest of is **[one project]**. **[One number or proof: users, revenue, time saved, growth rate, with a screen or link on screen]**. I'm showing it because **[one sentence: why it proves you can do this]**."

Fill this from your real experience. A single concrete number beats three vague claims.

## 3. Why this business will win (1:15 - 2:15)

- **Who it's for:** small teams, agencies and accountants' clients who file expense claims with receipts in a camera roll, a chat thread, or a shared inbox.
- **The problem:** claims arrive messy, get re-typed by hand, nobody checks policy until month-end, duplicates slip through.
- **Why not just Claude?** Claude can read a receipt, but the chat ends. It can't hold a team's claims, show a manager a queue, remember last week's lunch to catch a duplicate, enforce limits the same way every time, keep an audit trail, or give finance a CSV. Claude does the reading; the app does what must be reliable and persistent. Policy checks here are plain code, so they are consistent and free.
- **Why it wins:** zero AI cost for me means I can offer a generous free tier and still earn margin. The customer's Claude plan pays for the intelligence.

## 4. Demo (2:15 - 4:15), keep it live, no cuts

1. (0:10) Show the live app on screen, logged in, dashboard empty.
2. (0:10) Switch to Claude with the Tally Claims connector enabled.
3. (0:30) Attach `demo/receipt_2_client_dinner_flagged.png`. Type: *"File this as an expense claim for Arjun. Client dinner."* Let Claude call the tool on screen.
4. (0:15) Read Claude's reply out loud: it reports the flags and gives a review link.
5. (0:20) Refresh the app. The claim is there, marked "Claude". Point at the red **Over limit** and **Alcohol** badges.
6. (0:30) Open the claim. **Edit it**: set the business purpose to "Dinner with Acme leadership team", change the category if you like, save. Show the history line "edited".
7. (0:15) Approve it with a note.
8. (0:10) Click **Export CSV** and show the file.

Close with one line: "Claude did the thinking. Everything you just saw lives in my app and keeps working after the chat ends."

Before recording: open the app 2 minutes earlier so it isn't asleep, and delete any test claims.

## 5. The money (4:15 - 5:00)

Say these as **assumptions**, because they are:

- **Cost to serve:** no AI cost. Hosting is a flat fee (a small server, about $7/month on a paid plan, check current pricing), not per customer. A team's claims are a few database rows, so my cost per customer is a few cents a month at most.
- **Price:** Free for up to 25 claims a month. **Team: $19/month**, up to 15 people, unlimited claims. Later, **Firm: $79/month** for accountants managing several client workspaces.
- **First 100 customers:** (1) 50 hand-picked bookkeepers and small agencies, free setup in exchange for feedback and a quote; (2) a 60-second receipt-to-claim demo clip posted weekly on LinkedIn and X; (3) listing in MCP and connector directories and posting in small-business and Claude communities; (4) every CSV export carries a "filed with Tally Claims" footer link.
- **Year 1 goal:** about 300 paying teams, roughly $68k annual recurring revenue (300 x $19 x 12).
- **Year 3 goal:** about 3,000 teams plus 100 accounting firms, roughly $780k annual recurring revenue, and expanding into approvals and payouts for the finance team.

Finish: "Thanks for watching. The live app, test login and connector link are in my submission email."
