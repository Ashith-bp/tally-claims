Connect Claude

Your private connector link. Anyone with this link can file claims into your account, so keep it secret.

  1. In Claude, open Customize → Connectors, click + Add, then Add custom connector.
  2. Name it Tally Claims, paste the link above, set authentication to No sign in, and save.
  3. In a new chat, turn the connector on, attach a receipt, and say: “File this receipt as an expense claim for Priya, it was a client lunch.”
  4. Come back here to review, edit, approve and export.
